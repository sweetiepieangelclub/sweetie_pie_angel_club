Meet Mina

Mina (she/her), founder of Sweetie Pie Angel Club, is an Iranian-American Breathwork Facilitator, Reiki Master, artist, emotional influencer, and vibe curator based between Bushwick and the online sphere. A Los Angeles native turned Brooklyn creative, she's been facilitating healing spaces since 2016. She formerly taught Kundalini Yoga before recognizing its cult dynamics, an experience that deepened her commitment to integrity, trauma-informed care, and authenticity in spiritual spaces.

Mina's work is queer-inclusive, collaborative, and playful. She sees clients privately (online and in Bushwick), teaches Reiki-infused Breathwork, and leads Reiki trainings. Sweetie Pie Angel Club isn't just about healing, but also about expression and connection, with upcoming workshops like breathwork into intuitive painting and other cross-disciplinary collaborations with local artists. Mina curates breathwork loft parties with her partner, as well as collaborates with Maha Rose, Reforesters Laboratory, and The Meadow Dumbo. She has been featured by global wellness collectives like DAYBREAKER, and has produced "Conscious Clubbing" events at Brooklyn staples like Signal, Earthly Delights, and Mood Ring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first session.

reiki therapy is a japanese healing art. in japanese reiki means, “invisible energy with miraculous power.” reiki allows the mind and body to return to its natural state of well-Being. in-person sessions involve lying down on a massage table and receiving touch therapy, as well as soothing subtle energy. distance sessions feel just as effective, and are done in the astral plane either live on zoom, and remote reiki blasts are completed within 72 hours with an emailed energy report.

clients typically feel a tangible effect of tingly vibrations in their body, as well as drop into a theta state, a deep meditative state between consciousness and sleep. in such a state, they relax so completely, entering into parasympathetic rest and digest mode where the body heals itself, accesses the subconscious mind, and heightens intuition and creativity. reiki sessions move stagnant energy, causing physical shifts, as well as help clients hear their inner guidance system, and propel mental, emotional, and spiritual momentum forward. taking it easy and practicing gentle aftercare is recommended after a session.

breathwork is an active meditation that provides a somatic release via an open-mouth two-part hale, one part exhale. the breath effects can resemble a plant medicine trip in its intensity and simple profundity. mina guides this really gently to help you slowly build capacity. it activates mental clarity, encourages self-acceptance, creates spaciousness, shifts perspective, and teaches resilience. mina plays uplifting electronic music that encourages a thoughtful journey that spirals upward.

mina offers in-person private sessions from her home studio in bushwick, as well as Maha Rose, and online.
